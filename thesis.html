The science behind the scenes

It started as a thesis.

Before PanPals launched on the App Store in January 2025, it began as part of an MSc in Behavioural Science at the London School of Economics, in June 2024. That is how we know it works.

Autonomy

Something to keep track of.

Select a personalised goal and track your own progress. It builds accountability and the intrinsic motivation that turns into discipline. This one correlates most with cooking more often.

Relatedness

Something to relate to.

The feed is where community lives. People post, give each other bon appétits and capture their cooking moments. Captions create trends and comments carry real support.

Competence

Something to compete for.

Hold your streak, get the most comments, be the weekly winner. Nothing wrong with a little kitchen sweat and friendly, chef-ly competition.

Through an extensive literature review of persuasive, behaviour change and self-tracking technologies, the study identified Self-Determination Theory as the most common and effective framework. During the study, users rated every feature we built. Social and community features, the feed, self-tracking and interaction, ranked highest and most consistently. Goal setting, reminders and streaks add value, but the signal is clear: people are most motivated by each other.
