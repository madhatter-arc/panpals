Remember when the internet felt fun and honest, and you could share small pieces of your day without thinking twice? PanPals brings that feeling back for whatever you cook. We built one big table for every kind of home cooking: not to collect perfect recipes, plan meals or count calories, but to celebrate real people in real kitchens making real food. We don’t need more recipes. We need motivation: a streak to keep and pals who cheer you on, even for mediocre pasta. Pull up a chair, show us your plate, and we’ll show you ours.

Like Strava, Letterboxd or Goodreads. But for whatever you cook.
