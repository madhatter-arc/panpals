About us

An unlikely story
of resilience.

Three childhood friends from Istanbul, one big table.

A behavioural science graduate, a mechanical engineer and an architect walk into a room and pretend they’re product managers, software engineers and UI/UX designers. For what? To make you cook more. Why? Sorry, we lied. It was actually an app we made for ourselves and our friends.

Then people sent it to other people, and suddenly it felt like one big table. Because we wanted to celebrate home cooking and bond over it. Even if it’s a sad sandwich. Or mediocre pasta. Or a very delicate charcuterie board.

We’re all just trying to cook one more time a week. Not every day. Just sometimes. To eat healthier, to save money, to have control over our ingredients. There are already too many recipes. We don’t need education. We need motivation. And no calorie tracking, please.

PanPals isn’t built by silver-spoon-fed dreamers. Cansu, Arda and Gözde juggled jobs and visas across London, Rotterdam and Vienna. The first “no” was Cansu’s supervisor telling her not to use PanPals as a thesis subject. She ended up getting a departmental award. The second to hundredth no’s didn’t stop us either.

No hundred random “what shall we do now” calls. Just one WhatsApp chat, staying informed, working hard and believing in what our pals told us: that they love the app.

Come join us. Celebrate home cooking. Bond over real food.
Your first Pals, Cansu, Arda and Gözde

“What a privilege to be exhausted by a challenge we’ve set ourselves.”
